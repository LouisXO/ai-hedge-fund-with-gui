三本书净值Book NAV

每本书从首笔成交前一个收盘起 = 100;SPY、QQQ 自 2026-09-21 收盘起 = 100,用复权价(含分红)。书的线是模拟器口径加上持仓应得的分红(模拟账户不发分红);合计按日收益连乘;竞价口径 = 每笔成交按当天开盘竞价价重记、扣监管费。Each book = 100 at the close before its first fill; SPY and QQQ = 100 at the 2026-09-21 close, on adjusted prices (dividends included). Book lines are the simulator NAV plus the dividends the positions were entitled to (the paper account pays none); the total chains daily returns; the auction basis re-prices every fill at that day's opening cross, net of regulatory fees.

三本书的规则The three books

长线综合因子 · $60,000 · 30 仓Long composite · $60,000 · 30 slots

每天给全市场打分,四个经典因子等权合成,排名进前 30 买入、跌出前 60 卖出。怎么打分见下面「长线书怎么选股」。Scores the whole market daily on four classic factors, equal weight; buys the top 30 and sells below 60. The full method is under “How the long book picks stocks” below.

内部人短线 · $30,000 · 20 仓Insider short-term · $30,000 · 20 slots

公司高管或董事在公开市场用自己的钱买入本公司股票(SEC Form 4)后,次日开盘买入,持有 5 个交易日卖出。入场用当天有效的限价单(前一天收盘价 + 3%),不用市价单:模拟器的市价单按开盘后的卖一成交,可能远高于开盘价;高开超过 3% 的不追,下一个交易日再试一次。When an officer or director buys their own company’s stock in the open market (SEC Form 4), the book buys at the next open and sells five sessions later. Entries are DAY limit orders at the previous close + 3%, not market orders: the simulator fills a market order at the first ask after the open, which can be far above the open; a name that opens more than 3% higher is not chased and is tried once more the next session.

SPY 核心仓 · $10,000SPY core · $10,000

被动持有 SPY。大盘股选股的九个规则都没有找到边际(S37),所以这部分资金只拿指数,不按规则卖出。Holds SPY passively. Nine large-cap stock-picking rules found no edge (S37), so this money simply holds the index and is not sold by any rule.

长线书怎么选股How the long book picks stocks

完全是规则,没有人工判断,也没有机器学习拟合。四个因子都是学术文献里存在了几十年的经典定义,参数在上线前写死。It is entirely rule-based: no discretion and no machine-learned weights. All four factors use textbook definitions that have been in the academic literature for decades, and every parameter was fixed before launch.

1 · 股票池1 · Universe

当天在交易的美股,过去 20 个交易日平均成交额至少 500 万美元。成交太少的股票进出成本太高,不进池。US stocks listed that day with at least $5M average daily dollar volume over the last 20 sessions. Thinner names cost too much to trade and are left out.

2 · 基本面过滤2 · Fundamentals filter

只用打分当天之前已经向 SEC 申报的财报(时点正确,不偷看未来),而且最近一份申报必须在 200 天以内。再排除三类会让比率失真的公司:市值低于 1 亿美元、总资产低于 1,000 万美元、股东权益不到总资产的 5%(权益接近零或为负时,ROE 和账面市值比都没有意义)。Only filings already public with the SEC on the scoring day are used (point in time, no look-ahead), and the latest must be under 200 days old. Three kinds of company are removed because their ratios break: market cap under $100M, total assets under $10M, and equity under 5% of assets (near-zero or negative equity makes ROE and book-to-market meaningless).

4 · 标准化4 · Standardize

每个指标当天在全池里先截掉最高和最低 1%,再减均值、除以标准差,变成 z 分。一个族的分数是族内指标的平均。Each measure is clipped at the day's 1st and 99th percentiles, then turned into a z-score across the universe. A family's score is the mean of its measures.

5 · 合成5 · Combine

综合分 = 四个族分数的简单平均,不按历史收益拟合权重。至少要有三个族有分数才参与排名。Composite = the plain average of the four family scores. No weights are fitted to past returns. A stock needs scores in at least three families to be ranked.

6 · 交易6 · Trade

每天收盘后按综合分排名。排名进前 30 且有空槽位就买入,每个槽位 = 书净值 ÷ 30;已持有的股票只要还在前 60 就继续拿,掉出前 60 才卖。前 30 和前 60 之间的缓冲区是为了减少来回换手。订单在次日开盘成交;空着的槽位留现金,不分给其他股票。没有止损(回测里止损降低了收益)。After each close the market is ranked by composite. A name in the top 30 is bought if a slot is free; each slot is book equity ÷ 30. A held name is kept while it stays in the top 60 and sold when it drops out; the gap between 30 and 60 cuts churn. Orders fill at the next open. Empty slots stay in cash. There is no stop-loss (stops lowered returns in the backtest).

这本书实际买到了什么What the book actually ends up owning

四个族等权,但实际持仓往往分成两群。一群是动量极强的股票:动量 z 分能到 4 以上,而其他族很少超过 2,所以一只股票只要涨得足够多,就能靠动量一项排进前 30(今天前 30 里有 17 只动量分 ≥ 3)。另一群是深度价值股,常见的是商业发展公司(BDC)和抵押贷款 REIT,账面市值比和盈利收益率都很高(今天有 9 只价值分 ≥ 1.5)。基本面过滤的作用是把动量尾部里没有盈利、没有资产的公司挡在外面。回测检验过:把这两群拆开、改成排名标准化、或只做纯动量,效果都更差(S29、S30),所以规则保持原样。The four families are equal-weighted, but the holdings usually split into two groups. One is extreme momentum: momentum z-scores can exceed 4 while the other families rarely pass 2, so a stock that has risen enough can reach the top 30 on momentum alone (17 of today’s top 30 have a momentum score of 3 or more). The other is deep value, often business development companies and mortgage REITs with high book-to-market and earnings yield (9 today have a value score of 1.5 or more). The fundamentals filter keeps unprofitable, asset-light names out of the momentum tail. Rank-based scaling, splitting the two groups, and a pure-momentum book were all tested and did worse (S29, S30), so the rule stays as it is.

已知的弱点Known weaknesses

  • 回测对 SPY 的超额收益不显著(主动收益 t 0.92),这正是要用模拟盘攒记录的原因。The backtested excess return over SPY is not significant (active t 0.92), which is why the paper record exists.
  • 行业不做中性化,会集中在某几个行业;测试过行业中性版本,alpha 几乎归零(S24)。It is not sector-neutral and can concentrate in a few industries; a sector-neutral version was tested and lost almost all its alpha (S24).
  • 动量在熊市反弹时会大幅回撤。一个预注册的影子版本(SPY 比两年高点低 20% 以上时停用动量)每天并排记录,不交易,评估点之后再决定。Momentum crashes in bear-market rebounds. A pre-registered shadow version that drops momentum when SPY is more than 20% below its two-year high is recorded daily beside it, untraded, to be judged at the evaluation point.
  • 模拟盘成交价是模拟器给的,小盘股的真实开盘冲击测不出来。Paper fills come from a simulator; the real opening-auction impact on small caps is not measured.
